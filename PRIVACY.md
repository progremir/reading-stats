# Privacy policy

*Reading Stats (the iPhone app) and reading-stats (the server). Last updated 20 October 2026.*

Reading Stats shows the reading time, books and highlights that your e-reader syncs to a
reading-stats server. It has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking of its own.

## What the app sends, and where

- The app talks only to the server whose address you type in. It never sends anything to the
  developer or to third parties.
- You sign in to that server with its username and password, the same as on its website. The
  server then gives this phone a widget token so the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets can show
  your stats. The address and token stay on your phone, in the app's own storage.
- Book covers and pages come from that server. Links that leave it open in Safari.

## Who holds your data

Your reading statistics, highlights and account live on the reading-stats server, and whoever runs
that server controls them. If you run your own, that's you. reading-stats is open source
(https://github.com/progremir/reading-stats): the server keeps data on its own storage, stores
passwords only as hashes, and shares it with no one. Each account sees only its own data.

## Deleting your data

Signing out removes the widget token from your phone; deleting the app removes the address too. To delete
your account and its data, ask the person who runs your server, or delete it yourself if that's
you. Devices and sessions can be removed from the account page at any time.
